- The "Book Now" button in the header now opens a booking pop-up right on the page, so visitors never have to leave what they're reading.
- The pop-up guides them through five quick steps — their details, their vehicle, any extra services they'd like added, a preferred date and time, and finally their email and postcode to confirm.
- If someone starts the booking and enters their name and phone but then closes the pop-up, their details are still quietly captured as an enquiry so no lead is lost.
- Completed bookings land on your usual thank-you page, and the pop-up closes with the × or by tapping outside it — reopening fresh each time.
